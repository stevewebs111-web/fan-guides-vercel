Toca Boca World, Gifts, Events and Playing Together

Toca Boca World is a game about making up stories. There is no score, no timer and no way to lose, just a colourful town full of places to visit and things to play with. This guide is about the parts that keep players coming back week after week, the gifts, the seasonal events and the fun of playing together.

Weekly gifts are worth collecting

Every week the game gives out free gifts at the post office, usually a piece of furniture, an outfit or a decoration. Some gifts stay available for a while and some disappear quickly, so it pays to check in regularly. Players who collect them over months end up with a much richer world than players who only open the game now and then. The gifts are also a good way to try new styles without spending anything, because you can test a new lamp or a new jacket in your world before deciding whether you like that look.

Seasonal events change the town

Through the year the game runs seasonal events, with Halloween costumes, winter decorations, summer outfits and more. The town itself changes a little with each season, and the event items often become favourites that players keep in their houses long after the event ends. If you are new, the best time to start collecting is during an event, because the themed items are usually the most interesting ones, and they give your world a character that the everyday items do not. A simple fan resource that explains the places and features in plain language is the toca boca free guide, which is useful if you are still learning your way around the town.

Playing together as a family

One of the nicest things about Toca Boca World is that it works well as a shared game. A parent and a child can build a house together, one choosing the rooms and the other choosing the furniture, or two friends can take turns making up a story with the same characters. Because there is no competition and no fail state, nobody feels rushed, and the game never interrupts a story with an advert or a paywall. That calm pace is rare in mobile games, and it is why many families keep the game on their shared tablet for years.

Making your characters your own

The character creator is where a lot of the personality of the game lives. You can change hairstyles, outfits, skin tones and accessories, and mix them freely, so two players rarely end up with the same cast. Try giving each character a small job in your story, a baker, a teacher, a gardener, and then build a place for them to work. The game remembers your characters, so your cast is ready every time you open the app, and adding one new character now and then keeps stories fresh without starting over.

A few habits that help

Save the houses you like, because you can build more than one and switch between them. Put the post office on your regular route so you never miss a gift. And turn the sound on, because the small sound effects make the world feel alive. If you want to know more about the studio behind the game, the Toca Boca page on Wikipedia tells its story, and the game itself is on Google Play. The world is yours to shape, and the best stories are the ones you make up yourself.
